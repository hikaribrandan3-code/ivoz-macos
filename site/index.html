A focused Mac utility

Speak once. Keep your flow.

iVoz is built for everyday writing, support replies, notes, and code comments. Your recordings are processed on-device, without sending voice or transcripts to a cloud service.

Local speech recognition

WhisperKit runs Whisper with Core ML on Apple silicon. Download the model once, then dictate offline.

Optional local cleanup

Use a local language model to remove filler words and polish grammar. Cleanup can be turned off.

Made for your workflow

Set a global hotkey, add custom vocabulary and snippets, choose a tone, and search or export your history.
